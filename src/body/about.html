How far to trust the scanner

Tested against the handbook's own answer key: on all 24 exercise ghazals (226 lines), Pritchett's meter fits 225 lines, and stacking names the right meter for 23 of 24 ghazals (the miss: Akbar's English "M.A.", which she scans unusually). On her printed syllable-by-syllable breakdown of Mir's Hindi-meter ghazal it matches 108 of 108 syllables.

Rules: Pritchett & Khaliq, chs. 1–3. Word lists verified against the same.

The poets

Ghalib and Mir are Frances Pritchett's transcriptions. Every other poet in the Poets list comes from Rekhta↗: the Urdu, Devanagari and Roman as Rekhta gives them, with each ghazal linked to its page there. Rekhta does not name a ghazal's bahr, so the engine reads it from the poem: the meter family that fits most of its lines, using the Roman for what Urdu spelling hides (iẓāfat, doubled letters, a poet's pen name). A ghazal it cannot settle is listed as unfiled. The few hand-checked ghazals (Faiz, Dagh, Jigar, Firaq, Hasrat) keep their verified Roman.
