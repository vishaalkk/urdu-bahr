The poets

Ghalib and Mir are Frances Pritchett's transcriptions. Every other poet in the Poets list comes from Rekhta↗, Sufinama↗ and Urdushahkar↗: the Urdu, Devanagari and Roman as given, with each ghazal linked to its source page. Rekhta, Sufinama and Urdushahkar do not name a ghazal's bahr, so the engine reads it from the poem: the meter family that fits most of its lines, using the Roman for what Urdu spelling hides (iẓāfat, doubled letters, a poet's pen name). A ghazal it cannot settle is listed as unfiled. The hand-checked and curated ghazals keep their verified Sean Pue transliteration.

The Persian ghazals (Sufinama) are checked against Ganjoor↗, which gives each poem's meter: a ghazal Ganjoor has links to its page there. The Persian scanner reads word weights from Sufinama's Roman and from Steingass's Persian-English Dictionary (1892). Full list below.

The sarangi in the Tarannum sound plays held notes from two recordings on Freesound: “SARANGI 1” by Johnnydhol↗ (Dhol Foundation Archive), licensed CC BY 3.0↗, trimmed to single notes; and “sarangi2” by sarman1234↗, public domain (CC0).
