How far to trust the scanner

Tested against the handbook's own answer key: on all 24 exercise ghazals (226 lines), Pritchett's meter fits 225 lines, and stacking names the right meter for 23 of 24 ghazals (the miss: Akbar's English "M.A.", which she scans unusually). Rules: Pritchett & Khaliq, chs. 1–3. Word lists verified against the same.
