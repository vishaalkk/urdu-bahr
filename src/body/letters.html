Letters

Every letter and mark the Transliterator reads, the Roman and Devanagari it writes for each, and a word that shows the sound.

Urdu is in Frances Pritchett’s Roman. Punjabi, Saraiki and Arabic spellings follow the Library of Congress romanization tables (ALA-LC). Each example’s Roman and Devanagari are written by the same engine the Transliterator uses. The small code under each name is the letter in Pritchett’s ASCII, the notation the engine works in (Settings › Show Pritchett ASCII script shows verses in it). To type a letter in Roman, use the Roman column: ṇ ḷ b̤ j̈ d̤ g̈, or the IPA ɓ ʄ ɗ ɠ.
