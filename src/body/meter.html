Meter

Every bahr is a tune. Hear it, then learn to recognise it.

● Counting bahr — one cell repeated; "3 or 4 feet" just means how many times, with the last often clipped. Count the pulses.

◆ Shape bahr — one fixed contour, no clean repeat. Don't dissect it; hold it as a melody.

Families of bahrs

Pick a family to hear its tune sung as dum (long) and da (short), then sing along as the syllables light up.
