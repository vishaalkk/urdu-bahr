Meter

Every bahr is a tune. Hear it, then learn to recognise it.

Adapted from Frances W. Pritchett & Khawaja Ahmad Khaliq Anjum, Urdu Meter: A Practical Handbook. Meter numbers, patterns and allowances follow her meter list (Handbook §6.1 ↗).

Pick a family to hear its tune sung as dum (long) and da (short), then sing along as the syllables light up.
