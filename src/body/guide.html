Tips & Features

How to Use

Helpful features and controls you might not notice at first glance.

Sound & Rhythm Controls (⚙)

Tap the gear icon (⚙) in the top right to customize how poetry rhythms sound:

  • Voices: Switch between synthesized tabla strokes (dhā · tā), real recorded tabla, spoken human takes (dum / da), or clean pitch tones.
  • Record your own voice: Choose your voice in Settings to record yourself saying dum and da once — the app will play back rhythms in your own voice.
  • Tempo (BPM): Use the speed slider to slow down complex meters for careful listening or speed them up to natural recitation tempos.
  • Foot cues: Adjust the pause between metrical feet, or enable the soft drum pulse to feel where each metrical measure begins.

Interactive Scansion Tricks

  • Change a word's reading: In the Scan tab, tap any syllable chip to open its word: pin one of its alternate readings, add or remove an iẓāfat or tashdīd, or stop it grafting onto the next word. The line rescans at once.
  • Hear a broken line: If an edit (or a typo) knocks the misra out of meter, ▶︎ still plays it as typed. Dashed chips carry the wrong weight for the partner misra's bahr; chips marked extra have no place in it.
  • Live edits: Edit or tweak words directly in the scan box to see the meter matches and syllable chips update in real time.
  • No diacritics needed: You can paste transliterated poetry in plain Latin alphabet (e.g. dil e nadan) — the engine normalizes spelling and maps it to classical meter rules automatically.

Comparing Bahrs in Ghazals

  • Cross-poet comparison: In the Ghazals tab, poems are grouped by their bahr. Expand a group to see how Ghalib, Mir, and others built completely different moods inside the exact same rhythm.
  • Pattern vs. Verse: In any reader view, you can play the abstract meter pattern by itself or play the poem’s line to hear how words stretch and fit the beat.

Instant Script Switching

Use the اردو, हिन्दी, and Roman buttons in the header to switch scripts at any moment. Your active page, scanned couplet, and position are preserved instantly.

Shareable Direct Links

As you browse meters, search ghazals, or scan couplets, the URL in your browser address bar quietly updates with the relevant parameters (?open=, ?q=, ?t=). Copy and share the URL with anyone to take them straight to that exact meter, search, or scanned verse.
