Rhythm Roll

A standalone piano-roll for Urdu metrical lines. Each block's width encodes syllable duration (long = 2 units, short = 1, flexible = hatched at 1.5). Click ▶ to hear a demo click track drive the playhead — that audio lives only in this demo page; the widget itself never touches WebAudio. Toggle "editable" and click a block, or focus it and press Space/Enter, to cycle long → short → flexible.
