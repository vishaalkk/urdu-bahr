# Tabla strokes (the tabla theka, src/js/15c-taal.js)

Single strokes, trimmed to the attack, faded, mono 80 kbps MP3 (from Freesound's HQ previews). The page carries them
re-encoded smaller (`scripts/build_tabla_strokes.py` → `data/tabla_strokes.json`). Credited on the About page.

- **Marius Miron (mmiron), pack "tabla bols"**, freesound.org/people/mmiron/packs/8162, **CC0** (public domain).
  ghe1–5, ghe_long1–2, ke1–3, na1–2, na_open, te1–4, re1, tun1–3, tin1–3 (his "tas"); sounds 130405–130430.
- **Ajay Srinivasamurthy (ajaysm), pack "Tabla Strokes"**, freesound.org/people/ajaysm/packs/10737,
  **CC BY 4.0** (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0): credit needed if shipped. Files prefixed `a_`
  (dha, dhin, tin, na, tun, te, tit, ka, ge, re); sounds 171900–171913. Cut from performances, so their tails are short.

Dha and dhin are also built the way a player makes them: a right-hand stroke (na / tin) and ghe together, from
separate takes.
